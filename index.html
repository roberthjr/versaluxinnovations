Soap dispensing

Soap is already within reach.

VersaLux incorporates an internal soap bladder, keeping your liquid soap within the bathing product itself. Squeeze to dispense and continue bathing without repeatedly reaching for a separate bottle.

  • Fill the internal bladder with the liquid soap you already use.
  • Squeeze to dispense soap into the product as you bathe.
  • Lather on the surface you're already washing with.

VersaLux is a working prototype. Capacity, refill details and usage guidance will be published as product development continues. [SOAP SYSTEM SPECIFICATIONS PENDING]

That question became a design, and the design became the VersaLux working prototype — a single product built to carry multiple bathing surfaces and its own soap.
